Privaro

A vault only you can open.

Privaro is a vault for your iPhone. What you put in it is encrypted on the phone itself and only opens with Face ID or Privaro’s own passcode. There’s no account, and nothing is uploaded.

Free to download. Needs iOS 17.6 or later. Pro is optional: one payment of $19.99, nothing to renew.

Encrypted on your iPhone. With a key that stays on it.

AES-256
Every photo and video in your vault is encrypted with AES-256, right on the device.
No account
No sign-up, and no Privaro server holding your photos.
Face ID or passcode
The vault opens only with Face ID or Privaro’s own passcode.
Works offline
