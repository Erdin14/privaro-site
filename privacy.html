Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026 · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Privaro (“Privaro,” “the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a private, encrypted photo and video vault for iOS, provided by Erdinc Zarbo, the Netherlands. This Privacy Policy explains how Privaro handles your information.

The short version:

Privaro is built to be private by design. Your photos, videos, albums, captions, and tags are encrypted on your device and never sent to us. We have no account system, we do not operate servers that store your content, and we cannot see what is in your vault. There are no ads and no tracking. Apart from what you choose to back up or share, the only data that leaves your device is what’s needed to sell and confirm the Pro purchase, which Apple and RevenueCat handle.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, see, or store your photos, videos, or any vault content.
  • We do not have user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your vault data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party SDK in the app is RevenueCat, for the Pro purchase (see “Purchases”). If you have chosen in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated statistics; these come from iOS, not from Privaro, and contain nothing from your vault.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your content stays on your device (and is encrypted)

Everything you add to Privaro (photos, videos, thumbnails, albums, captions, tags, and file names) is encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption. The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the iOS Keychain; it is protected by your device passcode, is never synced to iCloud Keychain, and is never included in device backups. Your Privaro passcode and biometrics control who can open the vault in the app. Encrypted files are stored in the app’s private storage with full file protection and are excluded from the device’s backups to iCloud or a computer.

Because encryption happens on your device and the key never leaves it (except as a copy locked with your recovery key inside an optional iCloud backup), we have no ability to access, decrypt, or recover your vault content. If you forget your passcode and lose your recovery key, we cannot recover your data for you.

Optional features that involve data leaving your device

These features are off by default and only operate when you choose to use them:

  • Encrypted iCloud Backup (optional, Pro). If you enable it, Privaro stores an encrypted copy of your vault in your own iCloud account. The data is encrypted on your device before upload; the key is wrapped using a key derived from your recovery key (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). We never receive your backup or your key. iCloud is operated by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Sharing / Export. When you share or export an item (including encrypted .privaroshare files or “Save to Photos”), that content leaves the vault as you direct. Where it goes and how it’s protected is then governed by the destination you choose.
  • Wi-Fi Transfer (optional). Lets you upload files from a browser on the same local network to your device; nothing in the vault can be downloaded this way. This connection is local and is not encrypted in transit, so only use it on a network you trust. Files are encrypted as soon as they’re imported into the vault. No data is sent to us or over the internet during a transfer.

Device permissions and why we ask

Privaro requests these only when needed, and the data is used on your device for the stated purpose:

  • Photos: to import the photos/videos you select into your vault, and to save items back out if you choose. If an item you pick is stored only in iCloud Photos, iOS downloads it from your iCloud account first.
  • Camera: to capture photos/videos directly into the vault, for the in-app camera, and, if you enable it, to capture a photo on a failed unlock attempt (“break-in” capture). Break-in photos are encrypted and stored only on your device; you can view and delete them at any time.
  • Microphone: to record audio with videos you capture in the app.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (biometrics): to unlock your vault. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS; Privaro never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Location (optional, “Location-Based Lock”): if you enable it, your device’s location is checked on your device to decide whether to allow unlocking at places you’ve marked as trusted. Your location is not collected, logged, or transmitted to us. When you pick a trusted place on the map, the map comes from Apple Maps, which Apple provides under its own privacy policy.
  • Local Network: for the optional Wi-Fi Transfer feature described above.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Privaro Pro is sold as a one-time purchase through Apple’s StoreKit / App Store. Apple processes the transaction and your payment; we do not receive your payment details. Purchase status is handled through your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

To check whether you have Pro, and when you buy or restore it, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a service that confirms App Store purchases. RevenueCat receives a random ID it creates for your installation (not your name or Apple Account), your App Store purchase information, a note when the Pro screen is shown, and the technical information that comes with each request, such as your IP address, device and system details, and app version. It never receives anything from your vault. We use this to give you the Pro features you paid for, to prevent fraud, and to see statistics about purchases. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interest in running the app. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

This website

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. privaroapp.nl is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., and a copy at privaro-site.vercel.app by Vercel Inc., both in the United States. Like any web host, they process the technical information your browser sends with each request, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested, to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse; Cloudflare may also ask your browser to report connection errors to it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a working, secure website. See the privacy policies of Cloudflare and Vercel.

Data retention and deletion

Your content stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting items moves them to “Recently Deleted,” and they are permanently removed after 30 days (or immediately if you choose). Deleting the app removes the vault’s files; iOS may keep some of the app’s Keychain entries on the device, but without the files they open nothing. If you enabled iCloud Backup, you can remove that from within the app or via your iCloud storage settings. Exported backups and shared files stay wherever you saved or sent them. Because we don’t hold your vault data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children’s privacy

Privaro is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. Because Privaro stores your content only on your device and we do not maintain personal data about you on our servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly by managing or deleting data in the app. For any questions or requests, contact us using the details below.

Erdinc Zarbo is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Where the GDPR applies, you can also object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in the country where you live.

Security

We use on-device AES-256-GCM encryption, the iOS Keychain for key storage, PBKDF2 passcode hashing, and iOS file protection. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and features like Wi-Fi Transfer that you choose to use have the limitations described above. You are responsible for keeping your passcode and recovery key safe.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We’ll revise the “Last updated” date above, and significant changes will be reflected in the app or on our website.
