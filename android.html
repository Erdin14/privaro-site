Privaro for Android.

The same encrypted vault as on iPhone, for Android 10 and newer. Everything in it is encrypted on your phone, and the app has no internet permission at all.

Free to download. Needs Android 10 or later. Pro is optional: one purchase through Google Play, for life.

Encrypted on your phone. And it never goes online.

AES-256-GCM
Every photo and video, and the vault’s whole index, is encrypted on your phone, as it is on iPhone.
No internet permission
The app doesn’t have Android’s permission to use the internet, so it can’t go online, not even to us.
Passcode and secure hardware
Your passcode is part of the encryption, together with your phone’s secure hardware (Android Keystore).
No account
No sign-up, and no Privaro server holding your photos.

Works with your iPhone.

A password-protected backup made on an iPhone restores on Android, and one made on Android restores on an iPhone. Secure share files open on either.

Moving from iPhone

  1. On your iPhone, export a backup and give it a password. (On iPhone, backup export is part of Pro.)
  2. Copy the backup file to your Android phone.
  3. On Android, choose “Restore a backup” while you set Privaro up, or later in Settings > Backup & Restore, and enter the backup’s password.

Everything in the Android app.

Getting in

  • A passcode of its own

    Six digits, eight digits, or letters and numbers. After each wrong try, the wait before the next one grows.

  • Fingerprint or face

    Unlock with your fingerprint or your face. Privaro only accepts biometrics your phone rates as strong.

  • Recovery key

    If you forget your passcode, it opens the vault. You make it during setup (you can skip that step) and can replace it in Settings.

Keeping it hidden

  • Disguise app icon

    Hide Privaro behind a Calculator, Notes or Weather icon.

  • Calculator disguise

    Privaro can disguise itself as a calculator.

  • Decoy vault

    Set a second passcode that opens a separate vault with harmless photos in it. If you’re ever pushed to unlock Privaro, enter that one, and your real vault stays out of sight.

  • Duress wipe

    Set a passcode that erases the whole vault, decoy included, the moment it’s entered. It’s meant for the worst case, and it can’t be undone.

  • Privacy Mode

    Choose how strict Privaro is: Home, Away, Travel or Maximum.

If someone tries

  • Break-in capture

    After a wrong passcode, Privaro can take a photo with the front camera, so you know someone tried.

  • Security history

    Unlocks, failed attempts and exports, never your content, kept for as many days as you choose (30 by default).

  • Block Screenshots

    Keeps screenshots from capturing the vault.

  • Panic lock

    A button that locks the vault at once.

  • Face-down lock

    Turn your phone face down and the vault locks.

Your photos and videos

  • Albums

    Albums with subfolders and covers, and smart albums.

  • Per-album passwords

    Give an album its own password. It stays locked even while the rest of the vault is open.

  • Recently Deleted

    Deleted items wait there for 30 days, then they’re gone.

  • Bringing photos in

    From the system photo picker, from files, or with the in-app camera. Photos you take with the in-app camera never appear in your phone’s gallery.

  • Videos

    They play straight from the encrypted file.

  • Share and save

    Share a copy, or save one to your gallery, and clean off metadata such as location first if you like.

  • Secure share

    Send photos or videos to another Privaro user as an encrypted file that only opens with a passphrase you choose. Nothing passes through us on the way.

Making it yours

  • Lock screen themes

    Choose how the lock screen looks.

  • 17 languages

    Privaro speaks 17 languages.
