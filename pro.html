Privaro

Everything Pro unlocks.

Pro is a one-time purchase of $4.99. It lifts the limits of the free version and adds everything below, on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Nothing renews and there’s nothing to cancel.

Free or Pro, the basics never change: everything is encrypted on your device with AES-256, there’s no account, and only you can open your vault.

  • Unlimited storage

    The free vault holds up to 2.5 GB. Pro has no cap: the only limit is the free space on your iPhone.

  • Unlimited albums and folders

    Free includes up to 5 albums. With Pro you can make as many albums as you like and put folders inside folders.

  • Per-album passwords

    Give an album its own password. It stays locked even while the rest of the vault is open.

  • Location-based lock

    Only allow the vault to unlock in places you trust, like your home. Anywhere else, it stays locked even with the right passcode.

  • Decoy vault

    Set a second passcode that opens a separate vault with harmless photos in it. If you’re ever pushed to unlock Privaro, enter that one, and your real vault stays out of sight.

  • Duress wipe

    Set a passcode that erases the whole vault, decoy included, the moment it’s entered. It’s meant for the worst case, and it can’t be undone.

  • Break-in capture

    If someone enters the wrong passcode, Privaro can silently take a photo with the front camera and log the attempt with a timestamp, so you know if anyone has tried to get into your vault.

  • Screenshot and recording protection

    Stops your vault from being captured by screenshots, screen recording or AirPlay while the app is open, so nothing leaks to your camera roll or to a screen mirror by accident.

  • Disguise app icon

    Hide Privaro behind an everyday icon and name, like Calculator or Notes, so it doesn’t stand out on your Home Screen. Color icon variants are free; the disguises are Pro.

  • Encrypted iCloud backup and export

    Back up your vault to your own iCloud, or export it as a single encrypted file. Both are encrypted on your device before they leave, so no one (including us) can read them. Restoring a backup is always free, even without Pro.

  • Wi-Fi transfer

    Send files into the vault from any computer's web browser over your local network. No cable, no cloud, and no extra app to install on your computer.

  • Secure share

    Send photos or videos to another Privaro user as an encrypted file that only opens with a passphrase you choose. Nothing passes through us on the way.
